What to try

  • Go through the four onboarding pages and pick weekly or monthly.
  • Tap the jar or Add SunDrop, write a note, pick tags and drop it in. Watch the lid, the slip and the wink.
  • Add sample drops to grow the frog's sprout, then fast-forward a day to see the countdown change and the streak hold.
  • Open the gear and switch Appearance to Night.
  • Tap Shake to reminisce for a random memory.
  • Tap the chubby frog for a cheer.

This is a web approximation of the SwiftUI app. Haptics, real notifications, the in-app camera and on-device storage only work in the iOS build. Camera here opens your camera on a phone and a file picker on a computer.
